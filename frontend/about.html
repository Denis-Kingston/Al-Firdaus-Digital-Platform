About Al Firdaus

Dedicated to serving the community

Al Firdaus Institute & Mosque is dedicated to serving the community by providing Islamic education, a peaceful place of worship, and programs that benefit all.

Our Story

A decade of worship, learning and service

Founded to meet the growing needs of Muslims in Dar es Salaam, Al Firdaus began as a small prayer room and has grown into a full institute offering daily prayers, Qur'an memorisation, Islamic studies and community outreach.

Today, more than 500 students learn with us across 20+ programs, guided by qualified teachers and supported by a caring community of donors and volunteers.
