About Al Firdaus

Dedicated to serving the community

Al Firdaus Institute & Mosque is dedicated to providing authentic Islamic education, a peaceful place of congregational worship, and welfare programs that empower families in Dar es Salaam.

10+ Years of Service

Growing together in faith, knowledge, and brotherhood since our inception in Kinondoni.

Our Journey

A decade of worship, learning & community service

Founded to meet the growing needs of Muslims in Dar es Salaam, Al Firdaus began as a modest prayer space and has expanded into a full institute offering daily prayers, Qur'an memorisation, Islamic studies, and community outreach.

Today, over 500 active students learn across 20+ structured programs, guided by qualified teachers and supported by a dedicated community of donors and volunteers.
